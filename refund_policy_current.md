# Refund and Cancellation Policy (current version 3.0)

This policy replaced version 2.0 on 1 March 2026.

## Refund window
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans can be refunded in full within 7 days of the first payment.

## After the refund window
When the refund window has passed, no refund is given. The customer can still cancel at any time. The plan then stays active until the end of the period that was already paid.

## Duplicate or wrong charges
If a customer is charged twice or charged the wrong amount, ClipRank Labs refunds the extra amount in full. The customer must report it within 60 days of the charge.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after approval. Support cannot refund to a different card or account.

## Who can approve a refund
Support agents can approve refunds of up to $200. A refund above $200 needs approval from the support team lead.
