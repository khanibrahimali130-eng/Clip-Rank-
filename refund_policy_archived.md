# Refund and Cancellation Policy (archived version 2.0)

This policy was replaced by version 3.0 on 1 March 2026. It is kept only for reference.

## Refund window (old rule)
Under version 2.0, every paid plan, monthly or annual, could be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase.

## After the refund window (old rule)
After 14 days, an annual plan could be refunded for its unused full months, minus a 15% handling fee. A monthly plan could not be refunded after 14 days.

## Duplicate or wrong charges (old rule)
A duplicate or wrong charge had to be reported within 30 days of the charge to be refunded.

## Who could approve a refund (old rule)
Support agents could approve refunds of up to $100. A refund above $100 needed approval from the support team lead.
