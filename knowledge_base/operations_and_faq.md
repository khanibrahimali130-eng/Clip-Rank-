# Operations and Frequently Asked Questions

## Support tickets
Every support request becomes a ticket. Business customers get a first reply within 4 business hours. Pro customers get a first reply within 24 hours. Starter customers use the community forum and do not get ticket support.

## Support hours
The support team answers tickets Monday to Saturday, from 10:00 to 18:00 UTC.

## Escalation to the team lead
An agent must escalate a ticket to the support team lead when a customer reports lost data, when a refund is above $200, or when a customer mentions legal action.

## Data retention
Uploaded videos are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. Score reports are kept for the time shown in each plan description. When a customer deletes their account, all of their data is removed within 30 days.

## Clip limits
An uploaded clip can be up to 3 minutes long and up to 500 MB in size. The supported file types are MP4, MOV and WebM.

## Frequently asked questions
Can I change my plan? Yes. An upgrade starts immediately. A downgrade starts at the next billing date.

Is there a free trial? There is no trial for the Pro or Business plans. The Starter plan is free and has no time limit.

Can the tool score a live stream? No. The tool only scores short clips that are uploaded as files.
