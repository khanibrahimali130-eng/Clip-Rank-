# Plans and Pricing

## Plans at a glance
ClipRank Labs sells four plans: Starter, Pro, Business and Enterprise. All prices are in US dollars and do not include tax.

## Starter plan
The Starter plan is free. It includes 10 clip scores per month, 1 user and community forum support. Score reports are kept for 3 months.

## Pro plan
The Pro plan costs $29 per month, or $290 per year when paid annually. It includes 200 clip scores per month, 1 user and email support. Score reports are kept for 12 months.

## Business plan
The Business plan costs $99 per month, or $990 per year when paid annually. It includes 1,000 clip scores per month, up to 5 users, priority email support and the team dashboard. Score reports are kept for 24 months. Bulk upload of up to 25 clips at once is available on the Business plan only.

## Enterprise plan
The Enterprise plan has custom pricing. It includes unlimited users, a named account manager and single sign-on. Customers must email sales@cliprank.example to get a quote.

## Extra clip score packs
Any paid plan can buy a pack of 100 extra clip scores for $9. Extra scores are used after the monthly scores run out. They expire after 90 days.

## How the clip score is calculated
The score is out of 100 points. The title gives 30 points, the description 25 points, the tags 20 points, the thumbnail 15 points and the captions 10 points.
