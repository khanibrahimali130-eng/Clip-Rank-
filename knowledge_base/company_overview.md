# ClipRank Labs: Company Overview

This document is fictional teaching material.

## Who we are
ClipRank Labs is a software company founded in 2022. It makes the SEO Clip Score Tool, a web tool that helps creators and small marketing teams get their short video clips found on search engines and video sites. The company has 38 employees and works fully remotely.

## What the SEO Clip Score Tool does
A user uploads a short clip and types its title, description and tags. The tool also reads the clip's thumbnail and captions. It then gives the clip a score from 0 to 100 and lists the three changes that would raise the score the most.

## Who uses it
Most customers are independent video creators, social media managers and small marketing agencies. The tool is not made for live streams.

## Support hours
Customer support is available Monday to Friday, from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC. Support is closed on public holidays that are announced on the company status page.

## Contact
Customers can reach support by email at support@cliprank.example or through the help form inside the tool. Sales questions go to sales@cliprank.example.
